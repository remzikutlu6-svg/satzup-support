Account Deletion / Privacy Choices

Control your account

Delete from inside SatzUp

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile → Delete account.
  3. Read the permanent-deletion explanation and Apple subscription warning. Use Manage Subscription if you want to cancel renewal.
  4. Choose Permanently delete account. For an Apple-linked account, authorize with the same Apple ID if requested.
  5. Wait for deletion to finish. After the backend confirms deletion, SatzUp signs you out and returns to its signed-out screen.

You do not need to cancel a subscription before deleting your account. Opening this webpage does not delete anything.

What is removed

Deletion removes your authentication account, first-name/profile information, uploaded avatar, discovery card, partner requests and connections, blocks involving your account, reports involving your account, account-linked subscription entitlement records, and Apple deletion recovery state. Your account's learning preferences and progress on the device used to delete the account are also cleared where device storage is available. The action cannot be undone.

Local progress on other devices is not remotely erased. Remove the app from those devices to clear remaining app-local data, and manage device backups through Apple's settings. Public avatar caches can take time to expire.

What remains outside your account

Apple subscription notification deduplication records contain event identifiers, types and timing, without account references, and remain independently of your account. Service-provider logs, caches and backups follow provider retention settings. Apple controls its own billing and purchase records. SatzUp does not claim these are erased by its account-deletion action.

Apple subscriptions continue separately

Deleting your SatzUp account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Apple may continue billing until you cancel through Apple.

Manage your Apple subscriptions, or open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancellation and refunds are handled by Apple.

Apple sign-in and interrupted deletion

When Apple is linked to your account, SatzUp's backend revokes Apple authorization using a verified token. A fresh Apple confirmation may be needed. Use the same Apple ID that is linked to SatzUp.

If a step fails, the app shows an error and allows another attempt. Your avatar or Apple authorization may already have been removed even if the account still exists. A server-side recovery record allows the deletion to continue after a successful token exchange or revocation. SatzUp does not report successful deletion until the backend confirms it. If the response is interrupted, sign in again to check whether the account still exists and contact support if needed.

Other privacy choices

You can edit your first name, remove your avatar or discovery card, remove a partner connection, and control microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iOS Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for information stored on device, in the backend and by providers.

Deletion problems and contact

For account or deletion problems, describe what happened and include your app version and sign-in method. For purchase problems, include the product and the message shown. Never send passwords, verification codes, full payment details, or Apple sign-in tokens.
