Help with your German practice

About SatzUp

SatzUp helps you learn German through lessons, vocabulary, reading, shadowing, and guided speaking practice. Premium adds access to advanced learning content, guided AI Speaking, and practice-partner discovery.

Account and login

Use the same email or sign-in provider you used to create your account. Email, Google, and Sign in with Apple are supported. If Apple hides your email, your account may use an Apple relay address. Choose the same Apple ID when returning. For Google, select the account linked to SatzUp in the sign-in browser.

Keep the app open until sign-in finishes. If it fails, check your connection and try again. Apple sign-in requires a supported iOS device and Apple ID.

Email verification and password reset

After email registration, check your inbox and spam folder for the confirmation link, then return to SatzUp and log in. Use the resend verification action if needed; wait before sending another request. For an email/password account, use Forgot password and open the newest reset link. Choose a new password in the app. Expired links require a new request. Apple and Google passwords are managed by those providers.

Premium purchases

Purchases require an internet connection and your Apple App Store account. Wait for verification to finish. A pending purchase or an unverified transaction does not establish Premium access. If a purchase succeeds but access is missing, sign in to the SatzUp account associated with the purchase and restore it.

Restore Purchases

Open Profile → Subscription → Restore Purchases. This action is also available on the Premium screen. Use the Apple account that made the purchase. SatzUp verifies eligible purchases with its backend; an expired subscription does not restore active access.

Manage Subscription

Open Profile → Subscription → Manage Subscription, or open Apple subscription settings. You can also open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles billing, renewal, cancellation, and refund decisions for purchases made through Apple. SatzUp cannot cancel Apple's billing for you.

AI Speaking and permissions

AI Speaking provides guided phrase practice with prepared prompts and feedback based on recognized words. It does not provide an objective pronunciation assessment. Allow microphone and speech-recognition access when prompted. If recording does not start, open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition and enable SatzUp. Check that device restrictions do not prevent access.

Use a quiet space, speak after the microphone starts, and check your internet connection if recognition is unavailable. Recognition availability depends on iOS and language support. You can stop recording and try the phrase again.

Friends and practice-partner safety

Friends provides practice-partner discovery and connection requests. Publishing a discovery card makes your display name and German level visible to eligible signed-in learners. It is not a live calling or messaging service.

Use Block to prevent further discovery and requests between you and a learner and remove existing connections. Use Report for inappropriate profiles, harassment, spam, or other concerns. Reports are private; reporting alone does not block the learner. Do not publish sensitive personal details in your name or profile.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Delete account to review the consequences and confirm permanent deletion. Read the account deletion guide. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Contact support

For account or deletion problems, describe what happened and include your app version and sign-in method. For purchase problems, include the product and the message shown. Never send passwords, verification codes, full payment details, or Apple sign-in tokens.
