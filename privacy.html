Privacy Policy

Privacy in SatzUp

Effective: 3 October 2026

SatzUp is developed by Remzi Kutlu. This policy explains information processed by the SatzUp app and this support website.

Information we process and why

When you create or use an account, Supabase authentication processes your email, account identifier, sign-in provider, authentication credentials and session information. Email/password authentication uses Supabase's password handling. Apple or Google may supply account metadata, such as a name or avatar URL; Apple may supply a private relay email. SatzUp stores your chosen first name and optional uploaded avatar in its backend to display your profile.

Information supports signing in, maintaining accounts, showing profiles, providing Premium access, connecting practice partners, and handling safety reports. Information you send in a support request is used to respond to that request.

Authentication

Email, Google, and Sign in with Apple authentication use Supabase. Google sign-in opens the provider's sign-in flow; native Apple sign-in supplies an identity token verified by Supabase. Session credentials are retained on your device by the authentication SDK. Providers process sign-in information under their own policies.

For deletion of an Apple-linked account, a fresh Apple authorization may be requested. The backend exchanges the authorization code for a token and revokes Apple authorization. A restricted recovery record holds the refresh token and revocation status if a deletion attempt needs retrying. It is removed when the account is deleted.

Learning data on your device

Lesson and vocabulary progress, review words, XP, reading and shadowing completion, practice results, speaking progress, learning goals, experience and daily practice preferences are saved on your device, scoped to your account. Speaking preferences can include age, gender, German level and session duration; these preferences and shadowing personalization remain local. SatzUp does not upload this learning history or these preferences to its database for cross-device learning sync.

Account deletion removes the account's local preferences on the device used for deletion. Data saved on another device is not remotely erased. Removing the app can clear remaining app-local data; operating-system backups are controlled through your device and Apple settings.

Speech and microphone

Guided speaking uses the microphone and iOS speech recognition to obtain recognized words. SatzUp compares that text with prepared practice phrases and displays feedback. Recognized speech is held during the session; SatzUp does not save raw microphone recordings or transcripts in its backend or persist them as learning history. Completion information is saved locally.

Speech recognition is performed through the device's speech services. Depending on iOS, language and availability, recognition may involve Apple's servers. Apple determines its processing and retention. SatzUp also uses device text-to-speech for spoken examples. You can refuse or revoke microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iOS Settings.

Practice partners and Friends

If you publish a discovery card, the backend stores your account identifier, display name, German level and creation time. Your discovery name and level are visible to eligible signed-in learners, subject to access and blocking rules. Requests store the participants, status and creation time. Accepted requests represent connections.

Blocks store both account identifiers and a timestamp. Reports store reporter and reported account identifiers, a selected reason and creation time. Other learners cannot browse private reports. The reporter can see their own reports, and privileged backend access supports safety administration. Blocking removes existing connections and prevents pair discovery and requests. Reporting alone does not automatically suspend an account.

Uploaded avatars are stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket. Anyone with the file URL may access an avatar; avoid uploading sensitive images. Public file caches can take time to expire after removal.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles payment details. SatzUp receives purchase verification data and stores account-linked transaction identifiers, product, environment, subscription status, expiry or revocation dates, and notification information to verify Premium access. It does not receive your complete payment card details.

The backend also records Apple notification identifiers, types, environment and receipt times to prevent duplicate processing. These event records have no account foreign key and do not contain account email or purchase transaction identifiers. Deleting SatzUp does not cancel Apple billing; manage your subscription through Apple.

Service providers and international processing

Supabase supplies authentication, database, file storage and server functions. Apple supplies Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, subscription notifications and iOS speech services. Google supplies Google sign-in. GitHub Pages hosts this static support website.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own, depending on their infrastructure, account settings and your location. The app does not add advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The static support pages use no cookies, analytics scripts or contact form. Hosting and backend providers process requests and may keep operational logs, including network and request information, under their own settings and policies.

Provider policies: Supabase, Apple, Google, and GitHub.

Diagnostics

The app writes development logs for authentication and speaking, including recognized text in debug speaking sessions. Some purchase and avatar errors are also written to the device console, including outside debug mode. The release app does not integrate a remote crash-reporting or analytics collector. Backend functions may log operational subscription errors. Provider access logs and diagnostics are governed by service configuration and provider practices.

Retention and account deletion

Account data remains while your account exists unless you remove an avatar, discovery card or connection earlier. Profile, discovery, requests and connections, blocks, reports involving your account, subscription entitlement records and Apple deletion recovery records are removed when the backend deletes your account. The uploaded avatar is removed through the Storage service before account deletion.

Apple notification deduplication event records remain independently of accounts. Provider logs, caches and backups are not erased synchronously by the app's deletion action and follow provider retention settings. Apple retains and manages its billing records independently.

Use Profile → Delete account. Apple-linked accounts may require authorization with the same Apple ID. If a deletion attempt stops partway through, retry; an avatar or Apple authorization may already have been removed. Read Account Deletion / Privacy Choices for details.

Your choices and security

You can edit your first name, change or remove your avatar, remove a discovery card or connection, control microphone permissions, and delete your account. For questions about information associated with your account, contact support.

SatzUp uses authenticated access, database access rules and encrypted network connections. Privileged credentials remain on the server. No system can guarantee absolute security; protect your sign-in credentials and device.

Changes to this policy

This page may change as SatzUp's functionality and data practices change. The effective date identifies the current policy.
